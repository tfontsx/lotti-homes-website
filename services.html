What We Offer

Thoughtful spaces,
crafted with precision.

From high-end custom homes to renovations, extensions and complete kitchens, bathroom and laundry transformations, Lotti Homes delivers premium residential construction services across Adelaide and South Australia — with dedicated project management from first concept through to final handover.

01

High-end custom homes

Custom homes within the Adelaide region built around the way you live. From first concept to final handover, we manage every stage of your custom build — delivering a residence that's tailored, premium and built to last.

  • Custom architectural design
  • Knockdown rebuilds
  • Single & double storey
  • Fixed-price contracts
  • Premium fixtures & finishes
02

Renovations & extensions

Whether you're transforming a single space or extending your home's footprint, we deliver renovations and extensions that integrate seamlessly with your existing home — adding space, light and long-term value. From kitchens, bathrooms and laundries to full-home transformations, every detail is considered.

  • Full-home renovations
  • Heritage-sensitive updates
  • Ground-floor extensions
  • Rear & side extensions
  • Kitchen, bathroom & laundry renovations
  • Custom joinery & premium finishes
03

Project management & consultancy

Building or renovating a luxury home is a significant undertaking. We guide your project from early planning through to handover — coordinating consultants and trades, managing budgets and programme, and protecting quality at every stage so you can proceed with confidence.

  • Feasibility & early-stage consultancy
  • Budgeting & cost control
  • Programme & trade coordination
  • Design & approvals management
  • Quality assurance & site supervision
  • Residential development advisory
